Project Overview

This personal project explores the creation of a complete broadcast graphics and video production package for a fictional ARC Raiders esports tournament. The goal was to develop a cohesive visual identity while combining motion graphics, gameplay editing, 3D animation, and visual effects into a cinematic broadcast presentation.

For the opening sequence, I edited footage from three separate gameplay recordings, using carefully timed cuts, color and saturation adjustments, and animated graphics to create the illusion of three players entering the environment and preparing for the tournament together.

I also created animated electronic doors in Blender, incorporating smoke effects to enhance the transitions and overall cinematic presentation.

Additional work included lower thirds, scoreboard and HUD elements, tournament graphics, and animated transitions, with an emphasis on visual consistency, readable information, and broadcast-style presentation.
