Project Overview

I assembled equipment explanation notes covering technologies encountered in AV and broadcast environments. The reference brings together short descriptions of control systems, audio processors, switchers, encoders, routing panels, monitoring tools and signal formats.

Alongside these notes, I completed Viz University's Introduction to Vision Mixing with TriCaster training diploma in August 2024. My practical experience includes supporting and operating TriCaster-based live-production workflows.

Scope: These notes reflect learning and operational reference material. They do not represent original design, installation or programming of the equipment described.

Signal & Streaming Concepts

Video Transport

SDI — digital video/audio transport used in broadcast workflows.
NDI — network-based video connectivity between compatible devices.

Network & Streaming

SRT — transport for reliable low-latency video over variable networks.
HLS — HTTP-based streaming delivery.
DHCP — automatic network IP-address assignment.
