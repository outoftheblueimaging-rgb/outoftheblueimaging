A practical recording checklist created to support consistent capture, clean audio, test playback and careful media handling during scheduled gameplay recordings.

Project Overview

I authored an AJA recorder checklist for a gameplay recording workflow involving observers. The document consolidated PC preparation, recorder configuration, pre-recording tests, playback verification, scheduled recording procedures and media handling into one repeatable reference.

Its purpose was to reduce avoidable recording mistakes and make the operating process easier to follow.

Public portfolio adaptation: The steps below summarize my original checklist. They are not a universal setup guide for every AJA model or production environment.

Recording Workflow at a Glance

01 / PrepareCheck observer PCs and eliminate unwanted audio
02 / ConfigureVerify recorder settings and select the correct media slot
03 / ValidateRun test recordings and review playback
04 / RecordCapture matches and safely finish media operations

Configuration Reference

Settings specified in the original checklist for this particular workflow:

  • Video source: HDMI
  • Audio source: HDMI
  • Recording format: 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, ProRes 422 LT
  • File container: .MOV
  • Media file system: exFAT

These are project-specific documented settings, not recommendations for every recorder.

Pre-Recording Quality Checks

  • Disable PC notifications and unwanted music that could contaminate captured audio.
  • Confirm the recorder settings before formatting media.
  • Check the selected card slot before recording or formatting.
  • Monitor through headphones connected to the AJA recorder rather than the gameplay PC.
  • Confirm the intended gameplay monitor/source using the recorder display.
  • Make a test recording and check its playback before scheduled capture.

Live Recording & Playback Procedures

During the Schedule

  • Start recording five minutes before the scheduled game time to provide a buffer.
  • Stop recordings between matches or during intermission, as outlined in the original procedure.
  • Stop recording after the final match at the end of the day.

Testing Playback

  1. Stop the test recording.
  2. Press Play to review the recorded clip.
  3. Return to the live input view before resuming recording.

The original checklist describes using the Stop button twice to return to live view on the equipment used.

Media Handling & Risk Reduction

My checklist emphasized selecting the intended card slot, verifying that recording and playback had stopped, and waiting for the media slot indicator to turn off before ejecting the card.

It also documented where to find the recorder's media and format controls. Formatting erases media, so this public case study intentionally does not present the original steps as a general-purpose instruction to format cards.

My Contribution

I independently created the operating checklist based on the recording workflow I supported. The documentation captured settings, operator checks, test-playback steps and media-handling precautions in an accessible format.

My contribution was in operating, supporting and documenting the existing recording process—not designing or engineering the underlying AV installation.
