Equipment Spotlight — AJA Ki Pro Ultra 12G

Operated AJA Ki Pro Ultra 12G recorders to capture gameplay video and audio feeds from observer PCs during scheduled recording sessions. My workflow included HDMI input configuration, 1080p/60 fps ProRes 422 LT recording checks, media selection and formatting, test recordings, playback review, and safe media removal.

Workflow clarification: These recorders captured observer PC gameplay feeds, not direct camera feeds. PTZ camera and Shure wireless microphone troubleshooting were separate theater and AV support responsibilities.

Project Overview

I authored an AJA recorder checklist for a gameplay recording workflow involving observers. The document consolidated PC preparation, recorder configuration, pre-recording tests, playback verification, scheduled recording procedures and media handling into one repeatable reference.

Its purpose was to reduce avoidable recording mistakes and make the operating process easier to follow.

Original Documentation — Public-Facing Sample

I created a practical operator checklist for a scheduled gameplay recording workflow. This condensed presentation preserves the technical substance of my original instructions while omitting internal project identifiers.

AJA RECORDER / OPERATOR CHECKLIST Adapted documentation sample

01 / Prepare & Configure

  • Disable PC notifications and unwanted background audio.
  • Verify HDMI video and audio sources.
  • Confirm 1920 × 1080 / 60 fps, ProRes 422 LT and MOV recording settings.
  • Confirm the intended media slot before any media operation.

02 / Validate

  • Monitor audio using headphones connected to the recorder.
  • Check the intended video source on the recorder display.
  • Capture a test recording and review audio/video playback.
  • Return to live input monitoring before the session.

03 / Record

  • Begin capture ahead of the scheduled start for a buffer.
  • Manage recordings between matches and intermissions.
  • Verify the final recording has stopped at the end of the session.

04 / Protect Media

  • Confirm recording and playback are stopped before unmounting.
  • Wait for the card-slot activity light to turn off before removal.
  • Treat formatting as destructive; confirm the selected media first.

Portfolio adaptation of an original operational checklist. Settings and procedures were specific to the equipment and workflow supported, not universal AJA instructions.

AV Equipment Troubleshooting & Signal Verification

In the gameplay recording workflow, I checked observer PC video/audio signals, recorder inputs, recording media, and playback. I also performed camera, PTZ, and Shure microphone troubleshooting in separate theater and AV spaces.

Gameplay Video & Recording

  • Inspected, reseated and swapped video signal cables, including HDMI/SDI where applicable to the supported AV equipment.
  • Verified observer PC gameplay feeds reached the intended AJA recorder inputs.
  • Restarted equipment as needed and checked signal restoration.
  • Verified media status, recorder settings and successful test playback.

Separate Theater / AV Support

  • Verified wireless microphones were assigned to the correct channels on Shure receivers.
  • Checked microphone audio and signal routing when troubleshooting capture issues.
  • Checked microphone audio in theater/AV spaces and reviewed AJA gameplay recordings for clean audio in the recording workflow.

Configuration Reference

Settings specified in the original checklist for this particular workflow:

  • Video source: HDMI
  • Audio source: HDMI
  • Recording format: 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, ProRes 422 LT
  • File container: .MOV
  • Media file system: exFAT

Pre-Recording Quality Checks

  • Disable PC notifications and unwanted music that could contaminate captured audio.
  • Confirm recorder settings before formatting media.
  • Check the selected card slot before recording or formatting.
  • Monitor through headphones connected to the AJA recorder rather than the gameplay PC.
  • Confirm the intended gameplay monitor or source using the recorder display.
  • Make a test recording and check its playback before scheduled capture.

Live Recording & Playback Procedures

During the Schedule

  • Start recording five minutes before the scheduled game time to provide a buffer.
  • Stop recordings between matches or during intermission, as outlined in the original procedure.
  • Stop recording after the final match at the end of the day.

Testing Playback

  1. Stop the test recording.
  2. Press Play to review the recorded clip.
  3. Return to the live input view before resuming recording.

The original checklist describes using the Stop button twice to return to live view on the equipment used.

Media Handling & Risk Reduction

My checklist emphasized selecting the intended card slot, verifying that recording and playback had stopped, and waiting for the media slot indicator to turn off before ejecting the card.

It also documented where to find the recorder's media and format controls. Formatting erases media, so this public case study intentionally does not present the original steps as a general-purpose instruction to format cards.
