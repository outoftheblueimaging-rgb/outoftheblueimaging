Project Overview

I authored instructional documentation to help explain routine theater AV operation, including selecting presentation sources, using room controls, preparing microphones and adjusting lighting for a supported event or presentation.

Scope: My contribution was operational support and user-facing documentation for existing equipment. I did not design, install or program the Crestron control system.

Room Operation Workflow

A simplified, public-facing overview of the workflow documented for theater use.

01 / Prepare Check room readiness and presentation requirements.
02 / Select Choose the appropriate presentation source.
03 / Adjust Set lighting and microphone operation as needed.
04 / Support Monitor the session and address operational issues.

Operational Responsibilities

Presentation & Room Controls

  • Support presentation source selection and display readiness.
  • Use existing Crestron room controls for routine operations.
  • Help prepare the space for meetings and events.

Audio, Lighting & Support

  • Assist with microphone readiness and basic audio checks.
  • Operate available room lighting controls as needed.
  • Identify issues and coordinate escalation when appropriate.

Documentation I Created

The instructional material focused on repeatable room-use procedures, including presentation source selection, lighting controls, microphones and general operating steps.

The goal was to make everyday room operation easier to follow and support consistent preparation.

My Role & Scope

I worked with existing AV systems in an operational and support capacity and independently prepared explanatory documentation.

This case study demonstrates familiarity with room controls, user support and clear technical communication, rather than original AV engineering or control-system programming.
