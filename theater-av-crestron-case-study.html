Project Overview

I authored instructional documentation to help explain routine theater AV operation, including selecting presentation sources, using room controls, preparing microphones and adjusting lighting for a supported event or presentation. My theater support also included checking PTZ camera operation and verifying wireless microphone channel assignments on Shure receivers. I also performed basic troubleshooting of AV connectivity, rack cabling, and hardware resets to restore room functionality.

Scope: My contribution included hands-on operational support, basic connectivity troubleshooting, and user-facing documentation for existing equipment. I did not design, install or program the Crestron control system.

Operational Responsibilities

Presentation & Room Controls

  • Support presentation source selection and display readiness.
  • Use existing Crestron room controls for routine operations.
  • Help prepare the space for meetings and events.

Audio, Lighting & Support

  • Check wireless microphone readiness, verify channel assignments on Shure receivers, and perform basic audio checks.
  • Operate available room lighting controls as needed.
  • Check PTZ camera operation and video signal availability; identify issues and coordinate escalation when appropriate.

Hands-On AV & Connectivity Troubleshooting

In addition to operating the room controls, I performed basic troubleshooting when presentation equipment or network-connected AV devices were not communicating as expected.

Rack Cabling & Network Connections

  • Checked physical connections and performed rack-level patch-cable changes to help restore connectivity or connect equipment to active network ports.
  • Investigated basic connectivity issues affecting supported AV equipment and escalated problems when needed.

Hardware Resets & Signal Handshakes

  • Performed hardware power cycles and system restarts as part of basic troubleshooting.
  • Checked whether connected displays, presentation sources, and AV hardware re-established communication and signal handshakes after a reset.

PTZ Camera Checks & Video Verification

  • Checked PTZ camera functionality in theater spaces as part of room readiness and event support.
  • Verified camera video availability and checked connections when a camera feed was not working as expected.
  • Helped isolate camera-related operational issues and escalated faults beyond basic troubleshooting.

Shure Wireless Microphone & Audio Checks

  • Verified that wireless microphones were assigned to the intended channels on Shure receivers.
  • Checked microphone operation and audio signal availability during theater preparation.
  • Troubleshot basic microphone and receiver issues to help maintain dependable event audio.

Scope: These tasks involved supporting and troubleshooting existing systems, not configuring enterprise network infrastructure or programming Crestron controls.
