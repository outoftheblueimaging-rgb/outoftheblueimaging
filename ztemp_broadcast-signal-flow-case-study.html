Adobe Illustrator Signal Flow SDI / NDI TriCaster Audio / Video Routing Recording & Streaming

Project Overview

I created a broadcast-room signal-flow diagram in Adobe Illustrator using my own technical notes. My goal was to organize a complex collection of audio, video, switching, conversion, recording, and streaming systems into a readable visual reference.

The original drawing documented a specific operational environment. The illustration below is a simplified conceptual example, not a reproduction of its internal wiring or configuration.

Conceptual Broadcast Workflow

Illustrative only: This generalized flow is not an exact technical schematic and does not disclose private signal paths, device configurations, or network topology.

What I Created

  • An original Adobe Illustrator signal-flow drawing.
  • Organized video, audio, and production equipment into understandable paths.
  • Documented routing, conversion, recording, and streaming relationships from personal notes.
  • Created a reference useful for my own operational understanding and troubleshooting.

My Role & Scope

I supported and operated systems that had already been installed. My contribution to this project was observing, understanding, and documenting their operation—not claiming responsibility for the original system engineering, installation, or Crestron programming.

Technologies represented in my source notes include TriCaster, SDI, NDI, Crestron, Biamp, Softron, and streaming/recording equipment.
